3D-Printed VTOL Inspection Drone

Personal project · Structural design and CAD owner · In progress

An autonomous fixed-wing VTOL inspection drone, with four lift motors and one forward motor, built around a fully 3D-printed structure on carbon-fiber spars. Vertical takeoff lets it launch anywhere, while the wing gives it the range to cover large sites.

  • Taking components from concept through CAD to printed hardware.
